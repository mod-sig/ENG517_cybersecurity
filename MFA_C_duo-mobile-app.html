What Is Duo Mobile at NC State?

Duo Mobile helps NC State account holders verify their identity when signing in to university services by using a push notification or a passcode.

How Duo protects your NC State account

Your NC State Unity ID and password provide the first level of protection for your university accounts. Highly recommended, the Two-factor authentication (2FA) adds a second verification step. NC State uses Duo as its 2FA solution to help prevent unauthorized access when someone has obtained or guessed a password.

Duo Mobile is an app that lets you complete this second step on a mobile device. Instead of relying only on a text message, you can use the app to approve or deny a sign-in request with a push notification or generate a passcode.

Ways to verify a sign-in

Push notification
Duo sends a notification to your enrolled device. Review the request and approve it only when you initiated the sign-in and the details match what you expect.
Passcode
Duo Mobile can generate a passcode that you enter during sign-in. You can generate a passcode in the app even when your device does not have Wi-Fi or cellular service.

Why use the Duo Mobile app?

Text messages can be delayed or unavailable during an SMS outage, and attackers may intercept them. Using Duo Mobile provides another way to complete 2FA through push notifications or app-generated passcodes.

Recognize and deny unexpected requests

  • Approve a Duo push only when you are signing in and the request matches your activity.
  • Deny a push notification that you did not initiate.
  • Never share a Duo passcode or approve a request because someone asks you to.
  • NC State will never ask you for your password or security code.

An unexpected Duo request may indicate that someone else has your password and is trying to access your account. Deny the request and contact the NC State Help Desk if you are concerned about your account.

Set up or restore Duo Mobile

Download Duo Mobile from the Google Play Store or the Apple App Store. You must enroll a device before using the app for NC State 2FA. If you already use Duo Mobile and have a new or reset device, you may need to reactivate the app.

For step-by-step instructions, see Reactivate the Duo Mobile App or How do I add a new device to Duo?.

Get help

For help setting up or using Duo, contact the NC State Help Desk through the NC State IT Service Portal or call 919-515-4357 (HELP).

Important: This topic explains what Duo Mobile does and why it is recommended. It does not replace the step-by-step instructions for enrolling a device, using Duo during sign-in, or recovering access to an account.
