How to Enroll in DUO Multifactor Authentication

Enrolling in DUO MFA at NC State adds an extra layer of protection for your NC State unity ID.

You must have at least one device (mobile phone, tablet or security key) to begin.
Attention: NC State encourages use of the Duo mobile app as your primary authentication method for consistent reliability.
  1. Go to the Duo Enrollment and Device Management page.
  2. Log in with your NC State Unity ID and password.
  3. Follow the on-screen instructions to complete the enrollment process.
