What Is NC State's Virtual Private Network Policy?

NC State's VPN Acceptable Use Policy describes the purpose of the university's remote-access VPN, who may use it, and the responsibilities users must follow when accessing the NC State network.

What is a virtual private network?

A virtual private network (VPN) creates a private connection over a public network, such as the internet. NC State provides a remote-access VPN service for approved users who need to access resources on the university network from off campus.

Who may use the NC State VPN?

The VPN Acceptable Use Policy identifies approved NC State faculty, staff, and students as users of the university VPN. Users must have VPN privileges and authenticate with their NC State Unity ID and password.

The policy does not establish general VPN access for visitors or guests. Visitors should not assume that they are eligible to use the NC State VPN. A visitor who has separately been issued an eligible NC State account and VPN privileges should follow the instructions and authorization provided by NC State.

How is VPN service managed?

NC State describes VPN as a user-managed service. Users are responsible for selecting an Internet Service Provider (ISP), coordinating installation, and installing the required VPN software. Users are also responsible for obtaining and paying for basic internet service.

NC State provides Cisco Secure Client for VPN access. Users must install the client software to activate the VPN connection.

Authentication and access responsibilities

VPN access is controlled through ID and password authentication. For NC State faculty, staff, and students, the credentials are the user's Unity ID and password. Users must not share credentials or allow unauthorized people to access the NC State network through their VPN connection.

Network traffic and connection behavior

  • The VPN is an IP-only resource; other protocols are not supported.

  • Traffic destined for NC State networks is logged and associated with the user, as permitted by NC State's Computer Use Regulation.

  • Only traffic destined for NC State networks travels through the VPN tunnel. Other traffic travels through the user's ISP unless the full-tunnel VPN service is used.

  • VPN users are automatically disconnected after a predetermined period of inactivity. Users can sign in again to reconnect.

Enforcement and compliance

VPN use is governed by the VPN Acceptable Use Policy and the NC State Computer Use Regulation. VPN service may be terminated immediately if suspicious activity is detected and may remain disabled until the issue is identified and resolved.

An NC State employee who intentionally violates the VPN Acceptable Use Policy may lose VPN privileges. By using the NC State VPN, users agree to the policy's terms and conditions.

Visitors and guests

The VPN policy does not describe a general VPN connection procedure for campus visitors or guests. Visitors who need campus internet access should use the guest wireless network where available. Visitors who believe they require remote VPN access must obtain authorization and instructions from their NC State sponsor or the appropriate university IT support group.
